Installation, mandatory m-boot and verification

Mandatory m-boot is the intended prerequisite. Normal NIGHTLY adoption is not currently established: stable same-unit binding, a consistent complete snapshot, durable transaction/readback and an interruption-safe boot commit are missing or disabled. This page offers no boot write or workaround.

Ordinary app updates and first bootloader adoption are separate. A transfer, 100%, product name or USB reconnect does not prove adoption, successful boot, preserved data or playable sound.

The owner reported slow/black startup from 0.0.25 to 0.0.26, versus smooth startup from 0.0.25.1 to 0.0.26. A restart that stays stuck is a separate case. These paths need exact-model, exact-byte investigation and validation; no timing threshold or successful recovery is invented here.
