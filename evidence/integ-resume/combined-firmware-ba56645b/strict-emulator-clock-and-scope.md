# Canonical normal WIDE emulator clock and scope

The exact official fm1-live v0.5.1 binary runs with `--mhz 360`, `FM1_STRICT=1` and `FM1_NO_SKIP=1`. Its last-40-ms IRQ line prints `clock 2880 MHz` from the internal peripheral counter frequency. In strict mode the emulator stores eight sub-cycle ticks per emulated CPU cycle, so that counter advances at 360 × 8 million ticks per second. The guest CPU model remains 360 MHz. The peripheral clock divides elapsed ticks by this same scaled rate to report guest milliseconds. [Strict clock setup](https://github.com/joakimeriksson/fm1-emu/blob/c7e47c4/crates/fm1-live/src/main.rs#L299-L317) · [IRQ diagnostic](https://github.com/joakimeriksson/fm1-emu/blob/c7e47c4/crates/fm1-live/src/main.rs#L944-L957) · [Elapsed guest clock](https://github.com/joakimeriksson/fm1-emu/blob/c7e47c4/crates/fm1-soc/src/periph.rs#L431-L446)

The scored peak is firmware `x0x_dbg.max_us` in the terminal RAM dump. The firmware computes the render elapsed time from its 24 MHz TIMER4 tick difference, divided by 24. It records a late half and increments `audio_xruns` when the DMA free half changes during rendering. The last-40-ms IRQ instruction percentage is a separate emulator diagnostic. [Candidate render timers and DMA checks](https://github.com/gobbajogger/DnBOX/blob/ba56645b2142b134be3aeee2236406e1c95b2bde/firmware/src/app/main_fm1.c#L64-L112) · [Candidate timer scale](https://github.com/gobbajogger/DnBOX/blob/ba56645b2142b134be3aeee2236406e1c95b2bde/firmware/hal/fm1_time.h#L16-L33)

The verified saved projects use normal typed WIDE on Elite A and Pro B, and Synth One HIGH PASS/full Sub on the other owner. Each populated project uses 909, 808 and the 909FL BREAK user slot. The calibrated 922-event script requests PLAY at 4,934 ms and its last event is at 7,944 ms. The patterns continue to sequence after the physical stimulus ends. The shot requests 605,000 ms from boot, checks signal in each of the final 600 nominal one-second PCM bins and scores the full-window cumulative timing counters.
