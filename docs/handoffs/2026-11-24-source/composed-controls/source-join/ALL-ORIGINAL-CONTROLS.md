order is: finish source wiring for every authentic playable control in both
originals, compile that exact revision for Elite and Pro together with linking
disabled, then check both fit, then and only then run source/build behavior or
audio verification. No releases or device writes.

Each group closes from its app UI/callback evidence (original Build9 NIB/action
and receiver, or pinned public Synth One source) and the actual DnBOX control
input, value law and active receiver. Every slot is accounted for. A registered
AU identity, stored factory key, dormant outlet, or possible modulation
destination does not by itself establish a physical control. When the original
actually presents an editable control with intentionally inaudible behavior,
preserve its interaction and don't invent audio. Keep preset-only policy
separate in the Init-only controls build.
