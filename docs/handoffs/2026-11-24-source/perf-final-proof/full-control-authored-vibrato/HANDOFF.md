# Combined candidate authored vibrato

Apply full-control-authored-vibrato-source.patch against the exact full-control-candidate preimage. The matching host-test patch adds the mixed fixture and the exact bank witness. PREIMAGE.sha256 and POSTIMAGE.sha256 define the source delta. The combined candidate itself was read in place. The patch was built and tested from the isolated checkout in this packet.

Sound rows 6 and 7 have one owner each, derived from the active bass cell's engine and a validated marker. A SuperJ6 exact vibrato record owns its first 8 bytes. A Synth One record owns the marked 11- or 13-byte span. Unmarked bytes stay opaque across load, reset and preset switching. A new vibrato claim requires a free 8-byte prefix, and a malformed marked record is rejected. Synth One's existing rules for claiming its oscillator prefix and appended filter pair remain in force. The SuperJ6 factory transaction checks storage at admission and again before committing, so late occupancy cannot produce a partial typed preset. On replacement each engine clears only the validated span owned by the outgoing record. The reset path saves and restores precisely that span on failure. Older SuperJ6 knob markers keep using rows 4 and 5 for reading, and an edit upgrades them only when the shared prefix is available.

The existing no-inline Synth One and SuperJ6 knob commit functions remain in their original boundary arrangement. Undo validates and compares the one shared snapshot according to the source and destination owners. Existing note-lock payloads and format are unchanged. Vibrato follows the part's base sound. The combined undo candidate fits the prior Synth One buffer. Host size measurements confirm no change to the synth, core, chorus, undo candidate, sound or project. The decoder row grows by 8 B and the two factory mailboxes by 24 B total, as in the standalone authored integration.

The mixed fixture checks both assignments of SuperJ6 and Synth One and preserves all 32 peer-row bytes across transitions. It covers legacy knob/oscillator markers, full Synth One filter ownership, unowned trailer bytes, malformed markers, late opaque ownership, same-marker undo, cross-engine undo/redo and reset. The preimage failed both missing-authored-behavior and unsafe-admission assertions on both A and B. The exact bank witness loads all 640 records on both owners and tests held PCM, exact project reload, absence and signed zeros. All original audio and bank values remain local inputs. Generated fixtures are temporary.
