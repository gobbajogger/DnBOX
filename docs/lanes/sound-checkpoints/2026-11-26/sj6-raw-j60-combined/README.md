# Raw: host-only bridge direct/wet composition
Run bash sj6-raw-j60-combined/run.sh from the donor workspace with the historical private J60 available. Source hashes for the key donor scripts are in DONORS.sha256. Compiler custody is build.receipt. Binaries, PCM and impulse-derived coefficients are not in the archive.
The source full = isolated direct + isolated wet to at least −140.7 dBr on this cue. The fixed 43 taps approximate the independently clamped original direct to −62.1 dBr or better. Stock exactness is checked against the published donor tap. The four candidates are boolean choices of their own direct term (stock 1.3× vs live-generated source 43 taps) and real compact wet term (stock coefficient vs 0.2516540875455158), under the same chronological input and gain. The final JSON records same-input error normalized to the old source, and a clearly separate comparison to the newer full-AU terminal as context. They are not interchangeable references.
The combined candidate slightly improves the direct-only waveform but leaves the newer context center too hot by 1.52 dB at chord and 1.16 dB at tail. Even the candidate's same-input side residual stays above +3 dBr in those windows; retaining only the better wet post coefficient does not repair stage fidelity. Do not adopt this bridge based on its center alone. Other modes are untouched by construction: this is an offline one-profile ablation, not a dispatch or product patch. An authentic stopped-state invocation yielded a bit-identical passthrough. No native resource/timing claim.
