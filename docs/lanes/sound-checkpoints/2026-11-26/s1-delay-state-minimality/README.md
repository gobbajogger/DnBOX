# Synth One delay: exact-real state sharing and minimum order

Original tap recurrence writes x[n]+f[n]*prev[n−1], then reads its causal cubic tap with the same instantaneous delay d[n]. One long L and R delay use 2T; independently filtered right and long-R echo each feed a short T. For the two right short delays, the local read coefficients, circular indices and reset are identical. Adding their buffers and previous outputs preserves both recurrence updates and tap output over the reals by induction even with common active delay/feedback edits or tempo recalc; therefore only three linear histories are necessary: long L, long R, short sum(R+long R). This is the existing compact abstraction, not a newly available saving.

The previous echo is a one-audio-frame feedback state. For constant integer d, define w = z^-1, A_d = w^d/(1−f*w^(d+1)). Stereo transfer is diagonal: A_2T(L), A_T*(1+A_2T)(R). For constant legal 0<f<1, T positive integer, right numerator w^T*(1+w^(2T)−f*w^(2T+1)) shares no zeros with either denominator. At the roots of 1−f*w^(2T+1), the bracket is w^(2T), nonzero. At short denominator roots, f*w^(T+1)=1, bracket is 1+u^2−u with u=w^T; |u|>1 but both polynomial roots have |u|=1. The two denominator families have different radii. Left degree 2T+1 and right degree 3T+2; shared long poles require rank 2 since two independent I/O channels carry independent residues at each such pole. Therefore exact linear real state requirement ≥5T+3 in this legal fixed case, before general fractional controls. This is a recurrence-order proof, not a bit/byte lower bound for arbitrary finite precision or lossy coding.

Corner qualifications: at f=0 and constant integer T, yL=L[n−2T] and right yR=R[n−T]+R[n−3T]. A single right dry ring with two reads still needs 3T frames. On live two-stage retime the source cascade depends on its earlier tap control/interpolation (not just T[now]); no use of current-delay 3T replacement. Under provably identical signed L/R ingress, same long clocks and state from origin, the long lanes are identical and can share one. A poly/mono voice setting or momentarily equal input is not that condition; old stereo states are still audible during/after edits. Reuse only with independently guaranteed immutable mono source role plus retained history or cold reset before any genuine stereo change; no qualified product proof.
