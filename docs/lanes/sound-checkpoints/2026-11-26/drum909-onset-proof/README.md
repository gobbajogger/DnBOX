# First warm 909 cursor PCM divergence: narrow evidence, not adoption
No full replays were repeated. Direct raw archived captures are zero up to 65,599 inclusive. At 65,600 Elite baseline/candidate and Pro baseline become nonzero. Elite begins at different amplitudes, and Pro candidate's first nonzero is 66,624 (exactly 1024 frames later than baseline). Neither model's full PCM matches. The uninstrumented builds provide no per-half producer/apply/consume join and no first-cue owner or pre-master trace. The confirmed embedded engine-render trigger at d8_frame=65,536 dispatches RD, CH, 909 BD and 808 BD together, with BD applying master-pump keys, not Ride in isolation. There are repeats at render frames 69,632 and 73,728. Source only retains last marker at 73,728 and selected audio taps at that event; no first-cue render phase is bound to the capture frame, nor to the 2,220ms external tape boundary.

To test the most proximal changed mechanism, run the exact inherited warmed source before/after cursor patch (SHA 69e6f18b…/4c6ce4a0…) in focused standalone PI32, true generated 909 Huff sample assets (not synthetic sample bytes), actual drum909_init/trigger and serial full 909 four-bus renderer. Do not call this the full mixed scene: same authored trigger order/levels for Ride 1.0, closed hat .75 and 909 kick 1.0, no 808/master/DMA/remote, two trigger admissions (cold and retrigger after 512), eight 128-frame segments. 69,216 direct bytes matched across all eight spans: four float buses plus entire 6604-byte 909 owner per span (only four distinct relocatable sample pointer fields masked), including pos/fraction/huff bit/predictor/index/history/out state. Each segment was genuinely active. Both runs strict no skipped cycles. This rules out a mismatch intrinsic to that independently driven 909 path in the tested state, not application-admitted runtime state, target optimization/code placement, DMA delivery, 808 interaction or master. Do not align/clip raw PCM or attribute provisional onset to xrun solely by its two-half length.

To prove a cause requires same-boundary capture of exact applied owner/sequence/plan state, pre/post 909 decoder plus 909 and 808 bus witnesses, master pre/post words, and ALNK free-half at entry/exit joined to which DAC-read half/frame was captured. No current archive provides that join. Incumbent unchanged.
